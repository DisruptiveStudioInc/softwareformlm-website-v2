For a distributor, reaching a new rank may look like a simple change in their profile.

They had one rank before.

Now another one appears.

But to determine that this change should happen, the software may need to evaluate different conditions set by the company.

- Activity.
- Volume.
- Structure.
- Period.
- Requirements.

The new rank is the visible result of a process happening behind the scenes.

## Every company defines its own rules

Ranks do not work the same way in every MLM organization.

Each company can establish the criteria required to reach or maintain a particular position.

That is why the software needs to work with the organization's specific rules.

Before recognizing a change, the platform must be able to determine which conditions apply and whether they have been met.

## The system needs to consult information

To evaluate a rank, the software may need to review information from different areas of the platform.

- Is the user active?
- What volume do they record?
- What is happening within their organization?
- What requirements does the rank establish?
- During what period must they be met?

An isolated piece of data may not be enough.

The platform needs to connect the information involved in the established rules.

## Structure can be part of the evaluation

In some plans, reaching a rank does not depend only on individual activity.

There may also be conditions related to the distributor's organization.

This means the system may need to interpret information from different levels or relationships within the network.

Genealogy, then, can take part in processes that go far beyond showing who is connected to whom.

## Time can matter too

Some conditions may be evaluated within specific periods.

For that reason, it is not enough to know what happened.

It may also be necessary to know when it happened.

The platform needs to consider the periods defined by the company to determine whether the relevant conditions are met.

## Reaching and maintaining a rank can be different processes

Depending on each organization's rules, earning a rank and keeping it may require different evaluations.

This means rank management is not simply a matter of changing a label in a user's profile.

Behind it, there can be logic that needs to be reviewed constantly.

## What happens next?

Once the system identifies that the conditions have been met, the new rank can be reflected in the platform.

From there, that information may be relevant to other processes defined by the company.

That is why rank information should not be understood as a completely isolated element.

It is part of a larger system.

## Final reflection

When a distributor sees a new rank in their back office, they see the result.

Behind it, the software may have evaluated activity, volume, structure, periods, and different requirements.

Changing rank is not simply changing a name in a profile.

It is interpreting the company's rules and determining when they have been met.
